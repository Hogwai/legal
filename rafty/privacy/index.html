Privacy Policy

Rafty · Effective March 16, 2026

Rafty is a floating browser app for Android developed by Hogwai. All your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

Data Stored Locally

All data is stored exclusively on your device. Rafty does not transmit personal data to any server.

Data Details User Control
Browsing history URL, title, favicon, timestamp Incognito mode disables recording; can be cleared in-app
Bookmarks URL, title, favicon, optional note Can be deleted in-app
Saved sessions URL, title, favicon, position For session restoration; cleared on close
User preferences Search engine, bubble position, opacity, UI toggles, etc. Configurable in Settings
WebView data Cookies, localStorage, DOM storage from visited websites Managed by Android WebView

Rafty explicitly excludes all app data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Your data stays on your device only.

Network Transmissions

Transmission Destination User Data Sent
Web browsing User-visited websites Standard HTTP(S) requests (not collected by Rafty)
Ad-block hosts update GitHub (StevenBlack/hosts) None (anonymous GET request, every 7 days)
Premium purchase Google Play Billing API Handled entirely by Google Play
In-app review Google Play Review API Handled entirely by Google Play
App update check Google Play Update API Handled entirely by Google Play

Data Not Collected

Rafty does not collect, transmit, or process any of the following:

  • Analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising data or ad network interactions
  • User accounts or authentication credentials
  • Device identifiers (Android ID, advertising ID)
  • Geolocation data
  • Cross-app tracking data
  • Any data shared with third parties

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: all data is visible directly in the app
  • Right of deletion: history and bookmarks can be deleted in-app; uninstalling the app removes all data
  • Right of portability: data is stored locally on your device

Since Rafty does not operate any servers, there is no data processing on our side and no data processing agreement applies.

Permissions

Rafty requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Purpose
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Display floating bubble overlays on top of other apps
INTERNET Load web pages in the browser
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Check network availability for ad-block updates
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keep the overlay service running
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Run foreground service for floating overlay (required on Android 14+)
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Display foreground service notification
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Optionally exempt from battery optimization
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Auto-start search notification after device reboot

Children's Privacy

Rafty is not directed at children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR). The app does not knowingly collect data from children.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device and persists until you explicitly delete it (via in-app controls) or uninstall the app. Rafty has no server-side storage, so there is no retention period managed by the developer.

Third-Party Services

Rafty integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing (premium purchase): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play In-App Review: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play In-App Update: Google Privacy Policy
  • StevenBlack/hosts (ad-block list): open source project on GitHub

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please reach out via our contact form.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated via app update notes, and the effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
